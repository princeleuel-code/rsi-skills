# RSI Evidence-Governed Paid Growth

## When to Use
For explicitly authorized advertising accounts and campaigns.

## Procedure
1. Reconcile account identity, authorization, spend limits, conversion definitions, and revenue sources.
2. Discover intent and query waste.
3. Propose campaign, ad group, keyword, negative keyword, creative, and landing-page changes.
4. Challenge policy, attribution, budget, and causal assumptions.
5. Execute only within signed spend and resource boundaries.
6. Verify platform state and reconcile spend and revenue.

## Pitfalls
- Never auto-enable unsupported regulated-industry targeting.
- Never exceed signed financial exposure.
- Never claim revenue impact without reconciliation.

## Verification
Return budget receipt, platform receipt, conversion evidence, revenue evidence, and uncertainty.
