# ORBIT Ω Cross-Source Fusion

## Trigger
Use when multiple research sources contain fragmented, complementary, conflicting or differently scoped intelligence that must be synthesized into a stronger project-specific model.

## Procedure
1. Build a source ledger with identity, date, provenance, source class, scope, quality and freshness.
2. Extract atomic claims rather than fusing summaries as undifferentiated prose.
3. Label each claim observed, calculated, corroborated, inferred, disputed, stale or unknown.
4. Map agreement, contradiction, dependency, complementarity and missing bridges.
5. Preserve disagreements until evidence or scope resolution explains them.
6. Separate established mechanisms from hypotheses and design opportunities.
7. Find the strongest brittle point preventing the sources from composing into a useful system.
8. Generate candidate synthesis mechanisms, falsifiable predictions and required evidence.
9. Search for superior sources only through a separately authorized source-escalation mission.
10. Produce a project-specific synthesis with citations, uncertainty and next verification experiments.

## Verification
- every load-bearing claim retains provenance;
- no source is represented as stronger than its evidence class;
- contradictions remain visible or are explicitly resolved by scope/evidence;
- hypotheses are not stored as verified truth;
- proposed breakthrough has a falsification or evaluation path;
- private project data remains within allowed consumers.
